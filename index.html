Privacy Policy

crochetcreations24 SEO Studio · Last updated:

1. About

crochetcreations24 SEO Studio is a personal tool used only by the owner of the crochetcreations24 account to create and analyze Pinterest content for their own crochet products.

2. Data we access

  • The authenticated account's own profile, Pins, boards and their analytics.
  • Keyword metrics for the owner's own ad account, when connected.

3. Data we never access

We do not access or store data belonging to any other Pinterest user or third party, and we do not read private messages.

4. How we use it

Only to generate SEO titles, descriptions, keywords, tags and alt text, and to show Pin performance to the account owner.

5. Third parties

Product images and URLs submitted by the owner are sent to a hosted AI provider to generate text. No Pinterest user data is shared.

6. Storage

The app runs locally. Access tokens are stored in a private local file and are never published or shared.

7. Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with advertisers, data brokers or any third party.

8. Revocation

The owner can revoke access at any time from Pinterest account settings; after that the app can retrieve no data.
