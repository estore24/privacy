Privacy Policy

crochetcreations24 SEO Studio · Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. About

crochetcreations24 SEO Studio is a personal software application developed and operated by the owner of the crochetcreations24 Pinterest account. The application is used to create, optimize and analyze Pinterest content for the owner's own crochet products.

2. Pinterest Data We Access

When the owner connects their Pinterest account, the application may access information permitted by the Pinterest API, such as:

  • The owner's Pinterest profile information.
  • The owner's Pins and associated content.
  • The owner's boards.
  • Available analytics related to the owner's Pinterest content.

The application only requests and uses information necessary for the features provided by the application and the permissions approved by the account owner.

3. Data We Do Not Access

The application does not access Pinterest data belonging to other users. We do not access or read private messages, passwords, payment information or unrelated third-party account information.

4. How We Use Information

Pinterest information is used only to provide features such as Pinterest content optimization, SEO title and description generation, keyword suggestions, tag suggestions and performance analysis for the owner's account.

5. AI Processing

Images, product information, URLs and other content voluntarily submitted by the owner may be processed by an external hosted artificial intelligence service for the purpose of generating SEO-related text and content suggestions.

Pinterest account credentials and access tokens are not intentionally provided to AI services for content generation.

6. Storage and Security

The application is primarily operated locally by the owner. Authentication credentials and access tokens are stored locally and are not intentionally published or made publicly accessible.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or Pinterest account information. We do not share Pinterest account data with advertisers, data brokers or unrelated third parties.

8. Data Retention

The owner may delete locally stored application data and revoke Pinterest authorization at any time. Once authorization is revoked, the application can no longer access the Pinterest account through the revoked authorization.

9. Revoking Pinterest Access

The owner can revoke the application's access through their Pinterest account settings. After authorization is revoked, the application will no longer be able to retrieve information through that authorization.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features, data processing practices or integrations change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
